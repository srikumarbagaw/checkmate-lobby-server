Records exactly what this app is reading from the shared screen — the same video used for move detection, not your whole desktop. Start it before you begin playing, then download the file and attach it here if something needs debugging; it captures what the detector saw, alongside the text log.

Drag the corners so the box exactly frames the 8×8 board on the shared screen (no coordinates or border). The thin grid lines should fall exactly on the square boundaries.

Click "Start tracking" once, then turn on Auto-detect and leave it running — it keeps checking on its own, and when a single move can't explain what changed (usually because more than one move happened between checks), it automatically tries "Catch up" in the background too, applying it whenever it finds an exact match. You only need "Check for a move" or "Catch up" yourself if Auto-detect is off, or if it reports a guess that isn't exact and needs your OK. Typing the missed move below always works too.
